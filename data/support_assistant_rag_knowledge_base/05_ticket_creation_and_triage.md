# Ticket Creation and Triage
A useful ticket includes a concise title, problem description, expected and actual behaviour, reproduction steps where relevant, business impact, project/category and sanitized evidence.
Triage: clarify the issue, search for duplicates, select project/category, assess impact and urgency, set priority, route to the right team and ask for missing information.
Before creating a possible duplicate, show the likely match and ask whether the requester wants to add information to the existing ticket. Do not silently merge or close tickets. The assistant should summarize a proposed ticket and obtain confirmation before creating it.
