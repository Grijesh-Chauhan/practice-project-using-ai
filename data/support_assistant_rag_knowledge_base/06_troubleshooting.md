# Troubleshooting Guide
**Login:** Ask for account identifier and exact error; never ask for passwords or one-time codes. Route persistent failures through the approved identity-support process.
**Ticket not visible:** Check account, project membership and filters without disclosing restricted data.
**Cannot update:** Check role, permission, current status and allowed workflow transition.
**Attachment failure:** Collect file type, size, approximate time and error. Avoid unapproved channels for sensitive files.
**Slow application:** Collect affected operation, time, frequency, browser and correlation ID if available.
**AI/tool failure:** Do not fabricate success. Explain what could not be completed, preserve the request where safe and offer an approved alternative or escalation.
