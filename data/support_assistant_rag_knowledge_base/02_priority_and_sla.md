# Priority and Illustrative SLA Policy
These are fictional assessment values, not real company commitments.
| Priority | Meaning | Initial response target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Major outage or widespread impact | 30 minutes |
| P2 High | Key feature unavailable with significant impact | 2 business hours |
| P3 Medium | Limited issue with workaround | 1 business day |
| P4 Low | Minor inconvenience or general query | 2 business days |
Consider impact, urgency, affected users, business criticality and workarounds. A requested priority is context, not an automatic decision. Escalate when impact increases, a response target is at risk, or the team lacks expertise/access.
